There are two main ways to put an ad in front of Telegram users. **Official Telegram Ads** are bought from Telegram itself. **Channel promotion** means buying a post directly from a channel owner. Both work, but they suit different goals.

## How official Telegram Ads work

Telegram Ads are short sponsored messages that appear in large public channels, below the latest posts. You write up to 160 characters of text and add a button. You choose where the ad can appear by channel, by topic or, depending on your account, by audience characteristics, and you pay for impressions with a CPM bid.

Every ad passes Telegram's moderation before it runs, so the format is predictable and policy-safe. Reach is easy to scale: once an ad works, you can raise the budget without negotiating with anyone.

**Strengths:** scalable reach, precise channel and topic targeting, moderation built in, budget control.

**Limitations:** text only (no images in the standard format), strict ad policies, and the message is clearly marked as sponsored.

## How channel promotion works

With channel promotion, a channel owner publishes your ad as a regular post. It can include images, video and several links, and it reads like a recommendation from a source the audience already follows.

Prices are fixed per post or per period, and you agree them channel by channel. That makes it flexible, but also slower to scale, and quality varies a lot between channels.

**Strengths:** native format, rich creatives, strong trust in good niche channels.

**Limitations:** manual negotiation, inconsistent quality, and you must check each channel's real reach before paying.

## Which one should you start with?

- **Choose Telegram Ads** if you need steady, scalable traffic, your offer fits Telegram's ad policies and you want to control spend precisely.
- **Choose channel promotion** if your audience lives in a few niche communities, you need a visual or longer message, or your offer needs the trust of a channel recommendation.
- **Use both** for launches: channel posts create the first wave of attention, and Telegram Ads keep reach going once you know which message works.

## A simple way to test

Start with a small, equal budget on each. Use a tracked link for every channel and every ad, run them for one to two weeks and compare cost per click and cost per subscriber or sale. Then move the budget toward the better performer.
