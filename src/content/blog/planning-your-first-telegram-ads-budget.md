The most common mistake with a first Telegram campaign is not the budget size. It is spending it without a plan for what you want to learn.

## Decide what success looks like

Before choosing a number, choose a result: website visits, channel subscribers, bot starts or sales. Then decide what you can afford to pay for one of those results. That number guides every later decision.

## Treat the first budget as a test

Your first campaign is research. Its job is to answer three questions:

- Which audiences respond?
- Which message works best?
- What does one result really cost?

Plan a budget that lets each audience and message collect enough views to compare them fairly. Spreading a small budget across too many channels gives you no clear answer.

## Split it between formats

A simple starting split for many advertisers:

- **Most of the budget** on the format that best fits your offer (often official Telegram Ads for scalable traffic).
- **A smaller share** on a few well-chosen channel posts to test native recommendations.

Adjust after the first results come in.

## Track everything

Give every channel, ad and audience its own tracked link. Without this you cannot tell what worked. If you sell through a bot or a channel, track starts and joins as well as clicks.

## After two weeks: cut, keep, scale

Review the results. Pause audiences and ads that cost far more than your target. Keep the ones close to it and test small improvements. Increase the budget only on combinations that are clearly below your target cost.
