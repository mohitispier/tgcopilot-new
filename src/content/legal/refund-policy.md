## Managed services

- Before work starts, you can cancel an accepted quotation for a full refund of amounts paid.
- Once work has started, fees for work already completed are non-refundable. Unused ad budget that has not been spent or committed to a channel is refunded.

## Channel bookings

- If a channel owner declines or does not publish a booked post, you receive a full refund for that booking.
- If a post is published but removed earlier than agreed, you receive a partial refund in proportion to the time missed.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time and stay active until the end of the paid period. Partial periods are not refunded unless required by law.

## How to request a refund

Email [billing@tgcopilot.com] with your order or booking reference. We reply within [X] working days and process approved refunds to the original payment method.
