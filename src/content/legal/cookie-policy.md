## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. We use them to keep the site working and, with your consent, to understand how visitors use it.

## Cookies we use

- **Essential cookies:** needed for core features such as security and, when accounts launch, staying signed in.
- **Analytics cookies:** set by Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to measure visits and improve the site. These are used only where you allow them.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site from working.
